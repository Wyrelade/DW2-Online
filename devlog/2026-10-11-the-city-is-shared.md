# Other tamers in the city

*2026-10-11*

Digital City is now shared. When you walk into a room, you see the other tamers in it, each in
their own look, walking and running as they do on their own screens.

![Tamers in the Main Gate](../misc/screenshots/hub.jpg)

- Each tamer has a name tag: **Name Lv. N**. Staff have a coloured **[STAFF]** tag.
- When many tamers stand on one spot, the tags group up into one block ("and 3 others...").
- Rooms are split into channels so the city never gets too crowded to walk.
- The server knows the floor of each room. Moves through walls or faster than a tamer can run are
  refused.

## Drawing 49 tamers on a PlayStation game

The original game was written for 2 MB of memory and one player model. The first load tests with
bots ran out of memory at 8 tamers. The PC version now gives the game 12 MB, more texture slots
and a bigger video memory, and draws 49 tamers in one room without a hitch.

## When the connection drops

If your connection drops, the game holds with a **"Connection lost."** box. If it comes back
within the grace time, you continue where you were. Other players see you stand still for a few
seconds instead of vanishing, and a tamer who quits leaves their screen at once.
